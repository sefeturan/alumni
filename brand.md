# Alumni Network — Brand Guide

## Brand essence
Alumni Network is a welcoming, dependable professional community connecting graduates, students, and institutions. The experience should feel credible, optimistic, inclusive, and useful.

## Brand personality
- **Connected:** make discovery and participation effortless.
- **Trustworthy:** clear hierarchy, readable content, predictable interactions.
- **Ambitious:** communicate growth, mentorship, and career opportunity.
- **Human:** use warm, direct Turkish copy and approachable visual details.

## Voice and messaging
- Prefer concise, helpful, action-oriented language.
- Address users respectfully and inclusively.
- Use Turkish for product-facing interface copy.
- Avoid exaggerated claims; explain what an action does.

## Visual identity
- **Primary blue:** `#3157D5` — links, primary actions, focus states.
- **Deep navy:** `#172B4D` — headings, navigation, strong contrast.
- **Teal accent:** `#0F9B8E` — positive status and community highlights.
- **Canvas:** `#F4F7FB` — application background.
- **Surface:** `#FFFFFF` — cards, forms, content panels.
- **Border:** `#E5EAF2` — subtle separation.
- **Typography:** Manrope for headings; DM Sans for body copy and controls.
- **Shape language:** rounded 11–18px corners, restrained shadows, generous spacing.

## UI principles
1. Keep navigation consistent across every page.
2. Make the page title and primary action immediately discoverable.
3. Use cards for grouped content, not decoration.
4. Keep forms calm, labels explicit, and validation visible.
5. Ensure tables scroll horizontally on narrow screens.
6. Preserve accessible contrast, keyboard focus, and reduced-motion preferences.
7. Use responsive layouts that work from mobile to desktop.
